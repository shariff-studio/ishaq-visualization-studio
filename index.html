BEFORE WE BEGIN

Clear terms.
Confident collaboration.

Payment schedule

50% advance before project commencement and 50% before release of final high-resolution files. International payment method and any transaction or currency conversion fees are agreed before work starts.

Revisions and changes

Two revision rounds are included in applicable packages. Feedback must follow the approved brief. Major layout changes, new concepts, additional views and extra revision rounds are chargeable.

Client inputs

Clients should provide floor plans, dimensions, ceiling heights, reference images, material preferences, required camera views and delivery deadlines. Incomplete inputs can affect the quote and schedule.

Scope exclusions

Unless separately agreed, services exclude site visits, physical measurements, structural or engineering certification, government approval drawings, construction execution drawings, procurement, on-site supervision, animation, walkthroughs, VR and editable source files.

Resolution and file delivery

Final JPG/PNG files are delivered at the agreed resolution. 4K is included where supported. 8K output is subject to actual export capability and quoted separately. Editable project files require a separate agreement.

Quotation validity

Individual quotations are valid for 15 calendar days unless otherwise stated. Delivery timelines begin once complete project inputs and advance payment have been received.
